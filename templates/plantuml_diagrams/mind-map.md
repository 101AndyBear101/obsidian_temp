# Mind Map

## Purpose

Use to explore a topic as a hierarchy of ideas or categories.

## Diagram

```plantuml
@startmindmap
* Topic
** Area one
*** Detail
** Area two
@endmindmap
```

## Explanation

- **Root**: The central topic being explored.
- **Branches**: Major categories or sub-topics.
- **Sub-branches**: Deeper levels of detail.
