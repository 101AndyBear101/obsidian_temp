# Mermaid Diagram Templates

Use these templates with a `mermaid` fenced block. Start with the core relationship and planning diagrams; render advanced chart types in Obsidian before relying on them in wiki notes.

## Flows and Interaction

- [[workflow-flowchart-diagram|Workflow flowchart]] — Map steps, decisions, and outcomes in a process.
- [[service-interaction-sequence-diagram|Service interaction sequence]] — Show messages between services over time.
- [[service-state-machine-diagram|Service state machine]] — Define service states and valid transitions.
- [[user-journey-diagram|User journey]] — Capture a person’s steps and experience through a workflow.

## Models and Traceability

- [[class-relationship-diagram|Class relationship diagram]] — Model types, members, and their dependencies.
- [[entity-relationship-data-model-diagram|Entity relationship data model]] — Show data entities, fields, and cardinality.
- [[requirement-traceability-diagram|Requirement traceability diagram]] — Link requirements to their verification elements.

## Planning and Exploration

- [[project-plan-gantt-diagram|Project plan Gantt diagram]] — Schedule tasks, dependencies, and milestones.
- [[git-branching-graph-diagram|Git branching graph]] — Explain commits, branches, and merges.
- [[milestone-timeline-diagram|Milestone timeline]] — Summarize important events or phases.
- [[topic-exploration-mindmap-diagram|Topic exploration mind map]] — Explore a topic before splitting it into atomic notes.
- [[prioritization-quadrant-chart|Prioritization quadrant chart]] — Compare work using two decision criteria.

## Data and Trends

- [[distribution-pie-chart-diagram|Distribution pie chart]] — Show a whole divided into a few categories.
- [[flow-allocation-sankey-diagram|Flow allocation Sankey diagram]] — Visualize quantities moving between stages.
- [[trend-xy-chart-diagram|Trend XY chart]] — Compare values across an ordered axis.
